# Super Ultra Kenny Kart — PS3-style overhaul

This branch changes **Super Ultra Kenny Kart only**. It does not import, patch, or modify the Mode 7 / N64 / 3D Reaching stack.

## Included

- Standalone Super Ultra race engine behind the existing KARTS menu slot.
- 50 cinematic circuits arranged into 10 five-race cups.
- 24 selectable drivers.
- 12-racer fields (player + 11 CPU rivals).
- Quick Race and Grand Prix flows.
- Keyboard and joystick/gamepad input.
- Drift boost, boost strips, jumps, hazards, energy pickups, slip/contact behavior, and overdrive.
- Paged track browser and dedicated space-themed Super Ultra menus.
- Low rear chase camera with perspective road projection.
- Glossy layered player kart, rival detail pass, headlights, taillights, underglow and boost flames.
- Theme-specific landmarks: skyline, silo, bridge, torii, planet, volcano, mesa, factory, lighthouse and giant hog finale.
- Fog/bloom bands, horizon lights, weather/ash/rain effects, speed streaks, drift/boost particles and additional depth geometry.

## Playtest checklist

1. Open **Super Ultra Kenny Kart** from the Kenny Hoggins main menu.
2. Verify Quick Race can select all 24 drivers and browse all five pages / 50 tracks.
3. Verify Grand Prix exposes 10 cups and advances through five races.
4. Start a race and verify 12 total racers are represented.
5. Test keyboard steering/throttle/brake/drift/overdrive/pause.
6. Test a connected controller for menu navigation and race controls.
7. Check at least one circuit from each of the 10 visual themes for landmark/weather rendering.
8. Verify boost, ramp, hazard, energy, drift turbo and overdrive effects.
9. Exit back to the Kenny Hoggins menu and launch 3D Reaching to confirm it is unchanged.
